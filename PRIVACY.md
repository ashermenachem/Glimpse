# Glimpse GPA Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Glimpse GPA is an unofficial browser extension created by Asher Menachem. It shows an estimated GPA using the grades available to the signed-in student on MyMilken.

## Information the extension uses

On MyMilken, Glimpse reads the student's course names, course grades, course identifiers and term from the Progress page. It also uses an account identifier from MyMilken's account header to avoid showing a different student's cached grades. These details are needed to calculate and display the GPA and to carry the result between MyMilken pages.

Glimpse does not ask for or collect your password, authentication codes, or payment information. It has no account registration, ads, analytics, or tracking code.

## Storage and sharing

Glimpse processes grades locally in Chrome. It temporarily stores the latest course data and account identifier in Chrome's extension session storage. This session cache is available only to the extension, is not synced between devices, is not reused after 24 hours, and is cleared when Chrome restarts or the extension is reloaded, disabled, or removed. Glimpse does not send grades, the account identifier, or other student information to Asher Menachem or to a third-party server.

The extension may open MyMilken's Progress page in a background frame to refresh grades. That page communicates with MyMilken using your existing browser session and is governed by the school's policies. Glimpse's access is limited to `milkenschool.myschoolapp.com`.

## Your choices

You can remove Glimpse at any time through `chrome://extensions`, which clears its extension session data. You can also sign out of MyMilken. Glimpse is not affiliated with or endorsed by Milken Community School.

## Contact

Glimpse GPA was created by Asher Menachem. Contact the developer through [the GitHub profile](https://github.com/ashermenachem). Please do not post grades, account identifiers, passwords, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published in the [Glimpse GPA repository](https://github.com/ashermenachem/glimpse-gpa) with an updated effective date.
